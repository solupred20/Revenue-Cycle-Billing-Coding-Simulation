# Revenue Cycle, Billing & Coding Simulation

## Overview

This project simulates the complete healthcare revenue cycle workflow, including medical coding, billing, claim submission, and denial management.

It reflects real-world processes used in healthcare operations, insurance, and medical billing roles.

---

## Objectives

- Apply ICD-10 and CPT coding in clinical scenarios  
- Generate structured medical billing summaries  
- Simulate claim submission workflows  
- Analyze and manage claim denials  
- Demonstrate end-to-end revenue cycle understanding

5. Claim Submission

## Case Example

### Case 1: Acute Bronchitis

- ICD-10: J20.9  
- CPT Codes:
  - 99213 (Office Visit)
  - 71020 (Chest X-ray)

This case demonstrates outpatient evaluation, coding, billing, and claim processing.
Includes a dashboard-ready structure for revenue cycle performance analysis and KPI tracking.
---

## Denial Management

- Denial Code: CO-16 (Missing Information)  
- Root Cause: Incomplete documentation  
- Action: Resubmission after correction

# Revenue Cycle KPI Metrics

## Objective
Measure performance and efficiency of the revenue cycle process.

---

## Key Metrics

### 1. Claim Approval Rate
Definition:
Percentage of claims approved from the first submission.

Formula:
Approved Claims / Total Claims

Example:
2 Approved / 4 Total = 50%

---

### 2. Denial Rate
Definition:
Percentage of claims that were denied.

Formula:
Denied Claims / Total Claims

Example:
2 Denied / 4 Total = 50%

---

### 3. Resubmission Rate
Definition:
Percentage of denied claims that required resubmission.

Formula:
Resubmitted Claims / Denied Claims

Example:
1 Resubmitted / 2 Denied = 50%

---

### 4. Clean Claim Rate
Definition:
Claims accepted without errors on first submission.

Formula:
Clean Claims / Total Claims

Example:
2 / 4 = 50%

---

### 5. Average Reimbursement
Definition:
Average payment received per claim.

Formula:
Total Payments / Number of Paid Claims

Example:
(200 + 150 + 180) / 3 = 176.6

---

## Insights

- High denial rate indicates issues in documentation or coding
- Improving clean claim rate reduces delays
- Accurate coding increases reimbursement efficiency

---

## Conclusion

Tracking KPIs helps improve revenue cycle performance, reduce denials, and optimize financial outcomes in healthcare operations.

# Case 1: Patient Visit and Coding

## Patient Information
- Age: 45
- Gender: Male

---

## Chief Complaint
Persistent cough and shortness of breath for 5 days.

---

## Clinical Assessment
- Diagnosis: Acute Bronchitis

---

## ICD-10 Coding
- J20.9 → Acute bronchitis, unspecified

---

## Services Provided
- Office visit (evaluation and management)
- Chest X-ray

---

## CPT Coding
- 99213 → Office visit, established patient
- 71020 → Chest X-ray

---

## Summary
The patient was evaluated, diagnosed with acute bronchitis, and appropriate imaging was performed.

This case demonstrates basic outpatient coding for diagnosis and procedures.
